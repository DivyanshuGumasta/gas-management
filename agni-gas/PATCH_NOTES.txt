Agni Gas v0.4.5 update overlay

Extract the contents of this ZIP into your existing agni-gas directory,
the directory containing app.py. Allow files to be replaced.

This overlay adds damaged-cylinder dispatch and sole-active-supplier defaults.
It intentionally does not contain data/agni.db, backups, __pycache__, or .git.
Your local agency database and backups will therefore remain untouched.
